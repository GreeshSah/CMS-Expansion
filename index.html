Phase Strategy

Build a usable facility first, then complete the specialized interior zones.

Phase 1 creates the required project foundation: environmental approval, construction of the full building, finished office areas, and open facility space that can be used immediately. Phase 2 converts the open areas into a polished, sectioned advanced manufacturing facility.

Project Phases

The proposal begins with the required environmental review and a usable full-size building, then moves into interior build-out and future expansion phases.
