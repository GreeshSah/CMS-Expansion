Phase 1

Environmental Study & Building Shell

Phase 1 establishes the foundation for the CMS facility expansion. This phase includes the HUD-required environmental study, construction of the main 24,000 square-foot building, finished office and support space, and a large open common area that can be used while future interior sections are developed.

Primary Goal Complete required project startup

Conduct the HUD-required environmental study and prepare the project for building construction.

Building Scope 24,000 square-foot shell

Construct the full-size facility with finished office space, lobby, conference, and CAD/scanning areas.

Initial Use Open common area

Keep the remaining facility open and usable until the Phase 2 sectioned interior build-out is completed.
