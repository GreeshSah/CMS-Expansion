Interior Build-Out & Sectioned Facility

Phase 2 transforms the usable open building from Phase 1 into a fully sectioned advanced manufacturing facility. The plan organizes major training and production areas into dedicated spaces for robotics, machining, additive manufacturing, metal additive, reverse engineering, scanning, classrooms, offices, and support functions.
